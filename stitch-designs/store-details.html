About Greenfield

Our Heritage

Founded in 2011 as a single-stall market stand in Brooklyn, GreenField Organic Market has expanded to provide hyper-local, certified biodynamic and organic groceries to over 15,000 households.

Cold-Chain Assurance

Every refrigerated delivery leaves our store in insulated recycled tote bags with temperature monitoring tags, ensuring dairy and meat remain below 38°F.
